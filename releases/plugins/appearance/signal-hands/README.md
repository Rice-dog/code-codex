# Signal Between · 指间信号

An optional Canvas2D background with two hands drawn from a field of characters. A vertical scroll carries narrow streams of existing characters through the hands. Moving the pointer brushes, attracts or connects characters. Click for a ripple or hold Alt and scroll to adjust the hand pose. Motion, pose, pointer mode, pointer strength and scroll flow are configurable. The worker parks while the window is hidden and respects reduced motion.

可选的字符双手背景。上下滚动时，手形中的细列顺着滚轮方向流动；移动鼠标可以扫亮、牵引字符或拉出细线。点击产生涟漪，按住 Alt 滚动可调整手势。可在设置中调节动画、手势、鼠标效果及滚动数据流强度。

The generated hand texture and original renderer are bundled in the plugin. The visual inspiration is [Anthracite Studio](https://anthracitestudio.be/). The plugin renders offline.
